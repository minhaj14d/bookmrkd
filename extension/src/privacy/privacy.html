bookmrkd Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 May 2026 · Extension version

bookmrkd is a privacy-first bookmark library for Chrome. Your saved pages, tags, and search index stay on your device unless you explicitly enable optional features below.

Local library (default)

  • Saved bookmarks — URL, title, tags, favicon URL, and timestamps are stored in IndexedDB on your device.
  • No bookmrkd servers receive this data. No analytics or advertising trackers run in the extension.

Advanced — organize Chrome bookmarks

Optional bulk organize reads Chrome’s bookmark tree or HTML you import. Results are exported for manual import; live bookmarks are not modified automatically.

Advanced — Online mode (optional)

If you enable Online mode and save a Gemini API key, uncertain items may be sent to Google’s API as title + URL only. Google’s privacy policy applies to that traffic.

Cloud sync (v1.2, not enabled in v1.1)

Future optional sync will require login and transmit library data only when you opt in. v1.1.0 does not sync to any cloud service.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run analytics or external logging.
  • We do not upload your library unless you enable a future sync feature.
