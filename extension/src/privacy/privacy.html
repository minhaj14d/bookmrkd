bookmrkd Privacy Policy

Effective: 8 June 2026 · Extension version · Full policy on GitHub

bookmrkd is a local-first bookmark maintenance assistant for Firefox and Chromium-based browsers. Your data stays on your device by default. Optional features may contact third-party services only when you turn them on.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to bookmrkd servers. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising trackers.

Local storage (default)

  • Library bookmarks — URL, title, tags, favicon URL, timestamps in IndexedDB.
  • SCA feedback — accept/reject/ignore choices in IndexedDB for local learning.
  • Settings & optional API keys — in browser storage on your device.
  • Raindrop tokens — stored locally only if you connect Raindrop.

Browser bookmarks (AI Suggestions & Advanced)

Analysis reads your bookmark tree or imported HTML. Suggestions require your approval before anything changes.

Optional: local AI (MiniLM)

Local embeddings may download a model from huggingface.co and run inference on your device.

Optional: cloud AI (Gemini / OpenAI)

If you add an API key, title + URL (and similar short text) may be sent to Google or OpenAI. Use Local rules or MiniLM to avoid this.

Optional: Raindrop.io

If connected, the extension uses Raindrop’s API with tokens stored locally. Raindrop’s privacy policy applies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run analytics or external logging.
  • We do not upload your library unless you enable optional features above.
